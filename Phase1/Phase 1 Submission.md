# Paragraph:

_Cursed Cursade_ is a 2.5D action dungeon-crawler where the player controls a vengeful crusader (The Ashen Cross) who returns as a shadowed warrior haunted by past to infiltrate a massive corrupted fortress. Gameplay focuses on fast, skill-based combat, involving light and heavy attacks, evasive movement, blocking or parrying, and limited-use abilities that support both offensive and defensive playstyles. The player progresses vertically through **100 procedurally structured levels**, each containing randomized encounters such as enemy battles, minibosses, merchants, or healing areas. Every floor offers branching paths through multiple doors or stairways, forcing players to make strategic choices about risk and reward as they descend deeper into the fortress. Combat challenges intensify with each level, and the player unlocks powerful “boons” after clearing certain milestones, allowing gradual character enhancement throughout the run. The ultimate goal of the game is to survive the descent, grow stronger through each completed level, and reach the final chamber to confront the corrupted crusader warlord **Reynald the Unbound**, defeating him to complete the protagonist's cursed crusade of vengeance.
